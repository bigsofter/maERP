# s01 · chaos — "a trip lives in three places at once"   file: s01-chaos.html   sid: s01

Cues: T.sheet, T.chat, T.folder, T.truth, T.none. Scene opens the film (no camIn needed: the stage starts dark; start from nothing). Custom exit (see end).

Idea: ONE trip, split into three disconnected places. The viewer must feel the fragmentation, calmly.

Shot sequence

T.none − 0.1: the three threads fray: each thread's dash pattern breaks (animate strokeDasharray to gappy) and its opacity drops to 0.25; the three objects drift 20–30 px outward and desaturate (filter: saturate(.4) brightness(.8)); the empty dossier outline stays — holds until the exit. Tiny K.jitter on the three objects during the hold (amp 1).
7. Custom exit (T.out − 0.3 → T.dur): everything is sucked into the centre point: objects scale to 0.05 and translate to (960,540) with power2.in 0.75 s, slight motion blur (filter blur 0→6px), staggered 0.06; the chip shrinks into a bright point (white/blue glow 0→1) that remains at the very end (s02 starts from that point). Start the suck-in at T.out − 0.3 (it ends at T.dur). Title fades out at the same time.

SFX: pop at chip (in, off 0.5, gain .5); whoosh soft at sheet/chat/folder arrivals (gain .45 each); pop on chat_q bubble + chat reply; shimmer at truth (gain .5); swell at out−0.3 (gain .7, the suck-in).
