# s04 · the menu is built from role × modules   file: s04-roles.html   sid: s04

VO (ru): «{role}И меню не одинаковое для всех: оно собирается из роли сотрудника {modules}и из подключённых модулей. {sales}Менеджер по продажам не видит банк, {mech}механик не видит фактуры — {hand}и никто ничего не прячет вручную.»
Cues: T.role, T.modules, T.sales, T.mech, T.hand. Standard camIn/camOut. One title.

Product truth
- Visible items = what the employee's ROLE is granted ∩ the MODULES the company has switched on (same rule in the screens and in the API). Items of a module that is off are simply ABSENT (no padlock, no greyed row). There is no per-person manual hiding.
- Sales ({{ui:web.team_roles.sales}}) sees in Finance: {{ui:web.nav_factures}}, {{ui:web.nav_traites}}, {{ui:web.nav_previsions}} — NOT {{ui:web.nav_banque}}, {{ui:web.nav_achats}}, {{ui:web.nav_paiements}}, {{ui:web.nav_marges}}. (Sales DOES see invoices — never show Factures removed for Sales.)
- Mechanic ({{ui:web.team_roles.mechanic}}) sees only: {{ui:web.nav_missions}}, {{ui:web.nav_flotte}}, {{ui:web.nav_chauffeurs}}, {{ui:web.nav_carburant}}, {{ui:web.nav_maintenance}} (+ dashboard) — no {{ui:web.nav_factures}}, no bank, no clients.

Idea: a full menu "catalogue" passes through two filters and becomes two different personal menus.
Layout
- Centre at start: one ghost menu column ("all screens"), 360 wide, items 22 px: {{ui:web.nav_missions}}, {{ui:web.nav_flotte}}, {{ui:web.nav_chauffeurs}}, {{ui:web.nav_carburant}}, {{ui:web.nav_maintenance}}, {{ui:web.nav_factures}}, {{ui:web.nav_traites}}, {{ui:web.nav_banque}}, {{ui:web.nav_paiements}}, {{ui:web.nav_previsions}} — dark rail look, slate text.
- Two filter "gates" as horizontal glass bars across the column: first labelled {{copy:s04.role}} (with a person glyph), second labelled {{ui:web.nav_modules}} (with a puzzle/blocks glyph). They are chips on a thin line, not walls.
- Later: two result columns side by side (Sales left x≈380–760, Mechanic right x≈1160–1540), each topped by its role chip.

Shot sequence
1. camIn. T.role − 0.2: the catalogue column arrives centre (items stagger 0.05). T.role + 0.6: the {{copy:s04.role}} gate slides across the column top (line draws left→right, chip pops). Title {{copy:s04.title}} at T.role + 0.8.
2. T.modules − 0.1: the {{ui:web.nav_modules}} gate slides across just under the first one. Both gates glow once (shimmer). Hold.
3. T.sales − 0.2: the column duplicates into two (the copy slides left, the original slides right, K.MOVE 1.0 s); role chips pop on top: left {{ui:web.team_roles.sales}}, right {{ui:web.team_roles.mechanic}}. Left column: the rows Sales cannot see (Flotte, Chauffeurs, Carburant, Maintenance, Banque, Paiements) fade to 0.25 and collapse height to 0 (0.6 s, power2.inOut), the remaining rows close the gap. Just before collapsing, {{ui:web.nav_banque}} gets a brief amber outline (callout ①) so the eye catches "the bank goes".
4. T.mech − 0.2: right column: rows Mechanic cannot see (Factures, Traites, Banque, Paiements, Prévisions) collapse the same way; {{ui:web.nav_factures}} gets callout ② first.
5. T.hand − 0.1: both resulting menus settle with a soft check glyph on each role chip (green ok, not red). A cursor glyph with a small "hand-hide" gesture is NOT needed — instead the two gates fade to 0.35: the rules did it, nobody by hand. Hold to T.out.

SFX: whoosh at catalogue (.4), pop at each gate (2×, .5), shimmer at modules (.4), whoosh split (.45), soft click ×2 for callouts (.4), stamp soft on checks (.45).
